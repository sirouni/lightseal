Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · LightSeal (iOS) · 中文

This covers capture, sealing, checking files, and purchases.

We don’t host photos

LightSeal does not upload photos or video to our servers. Sealing and checking happen on the phone. This version has no server that stores your media.

Checking files is free and doesn’t go through us.

No accounts

No sign-in. We don’t sell personal data. Toggles live in UserDefaults and usually go away if you uninstall. Free-seal counts live in the Keychain and may sync with iCloud Keychain, so deleting the app does not reset the free allowance. Purchases stay with your Apple ID.

What leaves the device

These features talk to third parties. They typically see your IP and ordinary HTTP metadata — the cost of using the internet, not us selling your photos.

  • Apple App Attest: used when you seal, to show a genuine app. Photos are not sent to Apple as content.
  • App Store: Apple handles purchases, restores, and subscription status. Payment details don’t pass through us.
  • Place names (off until you ask): map previews are drawn by Apple Maps. Place names are looked up only after you tap “Show place names”; the photo’s coordinates are then sent to Apple Maps for reverse geocoding. The photo itself is never sent.
  • Trusted time (off by default, Pro): when on, a random 32-byte value is timestamped before the shutter, then a content hash after sealing. Defaults: Sigstore and FreeTSA. Only 32-byte hashes are sent, never the photo or video. If the network or authority fails, that shot fails.

What stays in the file

  • Capture record (always): the device clock time and time since the phone started, plus camera parameters: camera type, Make and Model, and for photos exposure time, aperture, focal length, ISO and flash; for video the configured resolution and frame rate and the exposure and ISO when recording started.
  • Location (optional): if “Embed location” is on, recent coordinates go into EXIF or the video’s QuickTime location atom and into the signature, together with accuracy, altitude when available, how old the fix was, whether full or approximate location was allowed, and whether iOS reported the fix as simulated or from an accessory. Location travels with the file you send. We don’t collect it separately. Off means we don’t request location.
  • Heading and context frame (Pro, optional): written into the sealed file for comparison. Heading uses the motion sensors (pitch, roll and compass direction) only while it is on. They don’t prove it wasn’t a recapture. The context frame is a low-res ultra-wide thumbnail that travels with the original, not to our cloud.
  • LiDAR depth (optional, off by default): on iPhones with LiDAR, rear-camera photos can carry a coarse depth map (at most 48 × 36 points, in millimetres) and the camera’s calibration data. Video and the front camera never record depth.
  • Network connection state (optional, off by default): whether the phone was online, which kind of interface was in use (Wi‑Fi, cellular, wired, other), and whether the connection was marked expensive or constrained. It is read passively from iOS: no request is made, and no IP address, network name or nearby device is recorded.
  • Trusted time tokens (Pro, optional): when trusted time is on, the authority’s signed timestamp responses are stored in the file.
  • Seal credential: includes a stable keyId that can correlate files from the same install.

Everything above is signed into the file and goes to whoever you send the original or envelope to. Turn off the optional items before capture if you don’t want to share them.

Permissions

Camera, Microphone (for sound in sealed video), Photos, and Location are requested only when needed. With the matching toggle off, you can still seal without that field. If location is on but there’s no fix, that shot fails. Heading reads the motion sensors, which iOS does not gate behind a prompt.

Analytics

No third-party ads or tracking SDKs. We don’t track you across apps. The privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types that require disclosure.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Turn off location, heading, context frame, LiDAR depth, network connection state, and trusted time anytime. Microphone access can be withdrawn in iOS Settings; video then records without sound.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings.
  • Uninstall the app.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月15日 · LightSeal（iOS）

本政策说明拍摄、签发、验文件和购买相关的信息处理。

我们不托管照片

LightSeal 不把照片或视频上传到我们的服务器。签发和验文件在手机上完成。本版本没有用来存你媒体文件的服务器。

验文件一直免费，也不经过我们。

没有账号

不注册。不出售个人数据。开关类偏好存在 UserDefaults，卸载后通常会删掉。免费签发次数记在钥匙串里，若开了 iCloud 钥匙串还可能随 Apple ID 同步，所以删掉 App 不会重置免费额度。购买状态跟你的 Apple ID 走。

会离开本机的请求

下列功能会向第三方发请求。对方通常能看到 IP 和常规 HTTP 元数据——这是上网的成本，不是我们把照片卖掉。

  • Apple App Attest：签发时向 Apple 证明正版 App。照片本身不发给 Apple。
  • App Store：购买、恢复购买和订阅状态由 Apple 管理。付款信息不经过我们。
  • 地名（点按后才查询）：地图预览由 Apple 地图绘制。只有点按「显示地名」后才会查询地名，此时照片中的坐标会发给 Apple 地图做反向地理编码，照片本身不发送。
  • 可信时间（默认关，Pro）：打开后，快门前对随机 32 字节盖时间戳，签发后再对内容哈希盖时间戳。默认机构为 Sigstore 与 FreeTSA。只发送 32 字节哈希，不发送照片或视频。没网或机构无响应时，这次拍摄失败。

留在文件里的信息

  • 拍摄记录（始终写入）：设备时钟时间和开机以来的时长，以及相机参数：摄像头类型、厂商与型号；照片另含曝光时间、光圈、焦距、ISO 和闪光灯，录像另含设定的分辨率、帧率以及开始录制时的曝光与 ISO。
  • 定位（可关）：开启「写入定位」时，最近坐标写入照片 EXIF 或录像的 QuickTime 位置原子，并计入签名，同时记录精度、可用时的海拔、定位时间距拍摄多久、授权的是精确还是大致位置，以及 iOS 是否报告该定位来自模拟或外接配件。位置留在你发出的原文件里，我们不单独收集。关闭则不请求定位。
  • 镜头朝向与环境小图（Pro，可关）：写入签发文件作对照，不能证明未翻拍。朝向只在开启时读取运动传感器（俯仰、横滚与罗盘方向）。环境小图是低清超广角缩略图，随原文件走，不上我们的云。
  • LiDAR 深度（可选，默认关）：在带 LiDAR 的 iPhone 上用后置摄像头拍照时，可写入一份粗略深度图（最多 48 × 36 个点，单位毫米）和相机标定数据。录像和前置摄像头不采集深度。
  • 网络连接状态（可选，默认关）：是否联网、使用的接口类型（Wi‑Fi、蜂窝、有线、其他），以及连接是否被标为高开销或受限。只被动读取 iOS 提供的状态，不发请求，不记录 IP 地址、网络名称或附近设备。
  • 可信时间凭据（Pro，可选）：开启可信时间时，时间戳机构签名的回执会存进文件。
  • 签发凭证：含稳定 keyId，可关联同一安装签发的多份文件。

以上内容都签进文件，你把原文件或信封发给谁，谁就能看到。不想分享可选项时，请在拍摄前关掉。

系统权限

相机、麦克风（为签发录像录制声音）、相册、定位只在需要时请求。关掉对应开关后，仍可不写入该项。定位开着却拿不到坐标时，这次拍摄会失败。朝向读取运动传感器，iOS 不为此弹出授权。

分析与追踪

本版本没有第三方广告或追踪 SDK，也不在其他 App 之间追踪你。隐私清单声明不追踪、不收集需披露的数据类型。

儿童

不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象。

你的选择

  • 可在系统设置里管理或取消订阅。
  • 可卸载 App。
