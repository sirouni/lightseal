What LightSeal does

You shoot in this app and write a seal. Someone else opens the original file in LightSeal to see if it changed after sealing, and whether this app sealed it.

A seal is a checkable mark. It doesn’t lock the file, block edits, or catch a photo of a screen. They need this app. iOS only.

Free

  • Checking files and exporting already sealed originals are always free, including after cancellation.
  • Free seals: 10 photos and 2 videos in total, granted once and not renewed, 60 seconds per video.
  • Free seals can include location if you turn it on. Heading, context frames, and trusted time each include one free capture trial; continued use requires Pro.

LightSeal Pro
